ALPHA BRAWL

P1: WASD move/jump   L-Shift block   F attack (+direction for tilts/aerials)   G special (+direction)
P2: Arrows move/jump   R-Shift block   . attack (+direction)   / special (+direction)
Character select on title screen. Press Enter to start / restart. First to knock the other off 3 times wins.

PIXEL SPRITES

Upload a sprite sheet per character. Sheets should be a vertical stack of 4 rows of frames — top to bottom: idle, run, jump, attack. Pixel-art crisp rendering is applied automatically. Sprites are saved in your browser (localStorage) and persist between sessions. Press ESC or click CLOSE to return.

EDITING

Set frames to 0 to fall back to the idle row for that state. Frame sizes > 96 px enable smoothed downscaling for AI-generated hi-res art; smaller sizes keep the crisp pixel-art look.
