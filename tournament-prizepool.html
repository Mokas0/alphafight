TOURNAMENT FORMAT

  • Single-elimination bracket — seeded by sign-up order
  • Early rounds: 1-stock matches (fast, ~60–90s)
  • Semis & final: 3-stock — earn the title
  • No character lock — switch fighters between matches
  • Parallel matches on multiple laptops to keep it moving

Bracket + payouts are posted publicly before round 1. Everyone sees the pool total and the split going in.

TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

  • Entries logged on a public list — name + paid ✓
  • Running pool total shown live (this calculator, projected on screen)
  • One named organizer holds funds; count verified by 2 players before payout
  • Pay-in closes before bracket reveal — pool is locked, no late math
  • Winners paid same day, in front of everyone

Community money only works on community trust. Everything is in the open.
