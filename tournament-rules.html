THE FORMAT

PHASE 1 — SWISS

  • 3 rounds, paired by record
  • Everyone plays all 3 rounds
  • No one is eliminated
  • 5 matches per round, run in pairs on the 2 stations

PHASE 2 — TOP-4 BRACKET

  • Top 4 by record advance
  • Single-elimination semis + final
  • Seed 1 vs 4 · Seed 2 vs 3
  • Winners face off in the Grand Final

EVERYONE GETS 3 GAMES MINIMUM · TOP 4 GET 1–2 MORE

MATCH RULES

⚔ 3 STOCKS
Standard match length. First to knock the other off 3 times wins.
⚔ ONE GAME PER MATCHUP
No best-of-three. One game decides it. Win or lose, you move on.
⏱ 4-MINUTE CAP
If neither player has 0 stocks at 4:00 → highest stocks remaining wins. If tied: lower damage % wins. Still tied: sudden death.
🎲 STAGE PICK
Loser of the previous match picks the stage from any of the 4 stadiums. Round 1 / first match: coin flip.
👥 CHARACTER DUPES
If both players want the same fighter, the higher seed (or coin-flip winner) gets first pick.

4 STADIUMS

LOSER PICKS · ALL 4 ARE FAIR GAME

CLASSIC
Original layout. No surprises. The default skill test.
LAVA LAKE
No void below — bottom is damaging lava that bounces you up.
JUBILANT JUNGLE
Animals drop in (20 HP each), attack you on contact.
CRYSTALLINE CAVES
3 small platforms drift to new positions every few seconds.

CHAMPION'S CHOICE: the Grand Final stage is picked by whoever finished higher in Swiss.

TIEBREAKERS

Used to seed the top 4 if multiple players share the same record.

1 ▸ WIN-LOSS

Most wins after 3 Swiss rounds. (3-0 beats 2-1 beats 1-2 beats 0-3.)

2 ▸ STOCK DIFF

Total stocks taken minus stocks lost across all 3 matches. Bigger number wins.

3 ▸ HEAD-TO-HEAD

If two tied players faced each other in Swiss, whoever won that match gets the higher seed.

4 ▸ STRENGTH OF SCHEDULE

Combined record of all your opponents. Beating tougher fighters earns the higher seed.

RUNNING IT CLEAN

FOR THE TOURNAMENT ORGANIZER

[1] Pick a TO who isn't playing. They handle pairings between rounds. Use Challonge or a Google Sheet — both are free.
[2] Lock in characters at registration. Players can change once at the top-4 cut. Mid-tournament switching kills the clock.
[3] Re-pair between Swiss rounds takes 60–90 seconds. Have it ready before previous matches end.
[4] Designate a "spectator station" while semis run on the 2 mains. Crowd watches there with commentary if you want it.
[5] If matches run long, drop Round 3 of Swiss → top-4 cut from a 2-round Swiss. Saves ~6 minutes.
